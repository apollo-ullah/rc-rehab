Sensors and car

The MuscleCar board reads both biceps and drives the real car over Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge).

    Real car

    Left arm turns right, right arm turns left, both go straight. One arm starting up to this long before the other still goes straight.

    In the Arm Grand Prix and demo, flex both to hold straight. Relax both to stop or start a new turn. After 8 seconds, relax both to drive again.

    Sit with your arms supported. A gentle squeeze is enough: the sensors only see whether the muscle is working, not how hard. Stop if anything hurts, and only add a weight if a therapist says so.

        How it works

        • Four units, each a different move: holding your head steady, turning left and right, opening and closing your jaw, and your lips.
        • Five lessons per unit: Find it, Hold it, Aim it, Quick and Mix. Each lesson is its own course with 10 gates. Do the move at a gate to open it. A miss still opens it, and the lesson ends at the finish line.
        • Boss races drive the real RC car using only that unit's move. You can also play them on this phone.
        • It adapts to you. Targets are a share of your own range. 90% or better makes the next go a little harder; under 60% makes it easier.
        • Rest is training. "I'm tired" always ends a lesson with credit. There are no lives to lose, and your weekly streak has rest days built in.

        Practise seated with good support, and stop if anything hurts or you feel dizzy. A therapist should choose which units fit you. The mouth units are control practice, not a treatment for speech, drooling or eating. Video never leaves this phone.
